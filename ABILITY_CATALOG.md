# Ability Catalog — all 52 abilities

Generated 2026-08-29 from the live asset data (`Assets/Data/PowerUps/`) as the reference
for the icon redesign. Rarities: **Common** (= normal), **Rare**, **Epic**. "Kind" is how
the ability behaves: *Passive* (always on once picked), *Consumable* (tap-to-use charge),
*One-shot* (armed passive that fires once), *Instant* (applies the moment it's picked).

## Common (15)

1. **Air Brake** — Passive — Eases the game's relentless speed-up: every future piece falls a little more gently, all run long. Picking it again brakes harder. *(Imagery: gentle slowing, air resistance.)*
2. **Domino** — Passive — Adds the small 1×2 brick to the falling mix. It stays rare, but now and then one drops in — handy for tidy two-cell fills and ledges.
3. **Extra Life** — Instant — Immediately grants one extra life, on the spot.
4. **Flip** — Consumable — Tap to instantly swap the block you're currently dropping with the next piece waiting in the queue — immediate access to a better shape.
5. **Foresight** — Passive — Widens the preview: you see the block *after* next, not just the next one, so you can plan placements further ahead.
6. **High Friction** — Passive — Blocks grip a little better on landing, sliding less on angled or uneven layers.
7. **Lighten** — Consumable — Tap to turn the falling brick feather-light: it drifts down slowly and lands softly, so you can thread it into a tight gap without bulldozing the stack.
8. **Pip** — Passive — Adds the tiny 1×1 brick to the falling mix. Rare, but perfect for plugging one awkward gap.
9. **Recovery** — Passive — Every time you lose a life, the next 3 blocks fall at half speed — a calm window to find your rhythm again.
10. **Shrink** — Consumable — Tap to flatten the current falling block down to a single 1×1 piece — an escape hatch from an awkward shape.
11. **Slo-Mo** — Consumable — Tap to halve the falling speed of the next 5 blocks, starting with the one already in the air.
12. **Slow Time** — Consumable — Tap and everything falls at half speed for 15 seconds — a stopwatch-style breather during fast phases.
13. **Vector Guide** — Passive — Projects a translucent landing preview under the active piece, showing exactly where it will come down.
14. **Ward** — One-shot — Holds a protective charge: the next hazard brick that appears is visibly struck down mid-fall and turned into a plain, harmless brick of the same shape.
15. **Zap** — Consumable — Your active block vanishes and a laser locks onto the column below; it charges for three seconds from a wide glow to a thin needle, then detonates the first block beneath it.

## Rare (22)

16. **Ballast** — Passive — Feather bricks (annoyingly light, drifty pieces) show up less often for the rest of the run.
17. **Bedrock** — Passive — Heavy boulder bricks join the falling mix: dense blocks that drop hard and weigh the stack down into place.
18. **Bomb Squad** — Passive — Bomb bricks (explode where they land) show up less often for the rest of the run.
19. **Dampener** — Passive — Tremor bricks (shake the whole tower) show up less often for the rest of the run.
20. **De-Icer** — Passive — Ice bricks (slippery, frictionless) show up less often for the rest of the run.
21. **Drag Chute** — Passive — Leans hard on the brake: everything falls noticeably slower and the level's speed-up takes much longer to bite. A stronger Air Brake.
22. **Last Stand** — Passive — While you're on your last life, blocks fall slower — pressure relief exactly when the stakes are highest.
23. **Liquefy** — Consumable — Tap to make the falling brick molten: on landing it pours like lava into the empty gaps beneath, then cools into solid stone — one piece fills holes and flattens the tower top.
24. **Locksmith** — Passive — Locked bricks (can't be moved or rotated while falling) show up less often for the rest of the run.
25. **Muzzle** — Passive — Maw bricks (fanged blocks that bite and devour neighbours) show up less often for the rest of the run.
26. **Overdraw** — Consumable — Three different shapes spawn at the top at once; cycle between them freely and choose which to drop first, second, third.
27. **Pocket Cache** — Passive — Unlocks a Hold slot: tap to bank the current block's shape and swap it with whatever is stored — your filter against bad luck.
28. **Rebound** — Passive — When a placed block topples off the screen, there's a 20% chance it teleports back into your queue instead of costing a life.
29. **Reinforce** — Consumable — Tap to turn the falling brick into a heavy boulder that drops hard and anchors a wobbling stack.
30. **Reroll** — Passive — Banks 3 rerolls: whenever ability choices appear, you can redraw all three cards. Saved across offers — spend them when it matters.
31. **Sacrifice** — One-shot — The first time a block falls into the abyss, it shatters *before* costing a life — but your topmost tower block shatters with it. One use.
32. **Sanitize** — Consumable — Tap while a hazard brick is falling to neutralise it into a fresh, plain brick of the same shape.
33. **Scrap** — Consumable — Instantly vaporizes the last piece you placed — erase a misdrop before it ruins the tower's balance.
34. **Steady Hands** — Passive — Vortex bricks (spin and drag pieces off course) show up less often for the rest of the run.
35. **Sure Grip** — Passive — A strong grip boost: pieces landing on angled or uneven layers barely slide. High Friction's big sibling.
36. **Suspension** — Consumable — Pick one placed block and sever it from gravity: it locks permanently in mid-air, even if everything under it disappears.
37. **Vine Bricks** — Consumable — Tap to turn the falling brick (and the one after it) into vine bricks that weld themselves to everything they touch — two in a row can glue a wobbling tower solid.

## Epic (15)

38. **Anchor Brick** — Consumable — Tap to turn the falling brick into an anchor: the moment it lands it freezes solid exactly where it is — permanent terrain, even if everything beneath vanishes.
39. **Anchor Drops** — Passive — Anchor bricks join the falling mix now and then: free footholds that freeze in place on landing.
40. **Brace** — Passive — Losing a life grants 10 seconds of immunity to further life loss — a whole cascade of falling blocks costs exactly one.
41. **Extract** — Consumable — Tap any block deep inside your tower to delete it — surgical correction of an old mistake without touching the layers above.
42. **Fission** — Consumable — Shatters the active block into independent 1×1 micro-cubes, one per cell; aim and flick each one down separately to pepper several tight gaps at once.
43. **Freeze** — Consumable — Instantly freezes every block placed so far: the whole tower locks solid in place.
44. **Guardian** — Passive — When a placed block topples off screen, there's a 50% chance it teleports back into your queue instead of costing a life. Rebound's big sibling.
45. **Hardline** — One-shot — A glowing laser line appears at the bottom of the screen; the first block that falls off your tower is caught by it and frozen in place — a mistake turned into an extended platform.
46. **Iron Will** — Instant — Immediately grants three extra lives.
47. **Magma** — Passive — Molten bricks join the falling mix: on landing they pour into the gaps beneath, then cool into stone — filling holes and scoring several cells at once.
48. **Overgrowth** — Passive — Vine bricks join the falling mix: they weld to everything they touch, gluing loose stacks into one solid mass.
49. **Purifier** — Passive — Every kind of hazard brick — Maw, Vortex, Bomb, Tremor, Ice, Locked — drops far less often for the rest of the run. (Only offered on hazard-heavy levels.)
50. **Slowburn** — Passive — Every new piece drifts down slowly for its first moment — time to read the board — then resumes full speed. A breather on every single piece.
51. **Titan** — Passive — All future blocks are heavy and grip hard: they plant firmly, resist shoves, and shrug off Tremor — but land with more force, so careless drops shake the stack.
52. **Updraft** — Passive — A constant updraft drags on every falling block: far slower descent, capped top speed, for the whole run. One pick, run-defining.
